Wildlife Removal FAQ

What animals commonly enter homes in Louisiana?
Raccoons, squirrels, bats and opossums are among the most common wildlife species that enter homes in the Baton Rouge area.
What should I do if I hear animals in my attic?
Scratching or movement noises in attic spaces often indicate wildlife activity and should be inspected by a professional.
Can wildlife damage homes?
Yes. Animals may chew wood, damage insulation and create openings that allow water or pests into the structure.
How do animals get into houses?
Wildlife may enter through roof vents, soffits, gaps in rooflines or openings around attic spaces.
Do you remove dead animals?
Yes. Salvant Environmental Services provides dead animal removal for animals that have died inside walls, attics or crawlspaces.
