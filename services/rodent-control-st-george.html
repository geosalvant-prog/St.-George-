Rodent Control FAQ

Are rodents common in the Baton Rouge area?
Yes. Rats and mice are common in Louisiana and frequently enter homes looking for food, shelter and warmth.
What attracts rodents to homes?
Accessible food sources, cluttered storage areas and small entry openings can attract rodents to structures.
How do rodents get into houses?
Rodents often enter through gaps in rooflines, vents, garages and small openings around utility lines.
Do rodents carry diseases?
Rodents can contaminate food and surfaces and may carry bacteria and parasites, which is why infestations should be addressed quickly.
What should I do if I hear scratching in my attic?
Scratching sounds in attics or walls are often a sign of rodent activity and should be inspected by a pest professional.
