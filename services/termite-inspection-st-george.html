Common Signs of Termite Activity

  • mud tubes along foundation walls
  • discarded termite wings near windows
  • hollow sounding wood
  • swarming termites during spring
  • damaged or blistering wood

What Happens During a Termite Inspection

Our termite inspection process is designed to detect both active termite infestations and conditions that could lead to termite problems.

Exterior Inspection

Foundation areas, crawlspaces, wood structures and exterior entry points are examined for termite activity.

Interior Inspection

Interior wood structures, baseboards, flooring and wall areas are inspected for termite damage.

Risk Evaluation

Moisture conditions and environmental factors that attract termites are identified.

Inspection Results

Homeowners receive a clear explanation of findings and recommendations if termite activity is detected.
