Does mosquito spraying really help?
Professional mosquito treatments can significantly reduce mosquito activity around outdoor living spaces when applied regularly and paired with good property maintenance.

Treatments usually focus on shaded vegetation, shrubs, fence lines, moisture-prone areas and other places where mosquitoes rest during the day.

No treatment can guarantee complete elimination, but professional mosquito service is designed to reduce mosquito populations and make outdoor areas much more comfortable.

Louisiana's heat, humidity and rainfall create ideal breeding conditions for mosquitoes, especially around shaded yards and standing water.
Can mosquito treatment help around patios and pools?
Yes. Mosquito control programs are commonly used to reduce mosquito activity around patios, pools, porches and backyard gathering areas.
